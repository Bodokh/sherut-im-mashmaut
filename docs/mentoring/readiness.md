General tasks/full calendar, public intake, documents, English, exports and military-specific fields are not silently represented as implemented requirements. Their release scope is D-09/D-12; no additional client questionnaire or approval is needed to finish authorized implementation and verification work.

A scope audit found no additional gap in settled staff workflows. This does not settle ancillary open scope, agreed client/device/volume acceptance, policy, operational rehearsal/handoff or hosted/provider proof. Do not describe the remaining work as credentials alone.

The provisional [protection contract](technical-spec.md#6-authorization-and-ceo-protection) protects supplied profile keys, assignment or lifecycle without creating an unrelated whole-participant lock. Delete/restore retain existing protection. CEO contact/correction/review still protects the contact object. A new completed contact preserves prior scheduling in immutable history and starts a new cycle; CEO `record_contact` does not add a permanent appointment lock. Existing whole locks, explicit schedule locks and standalone-reschedule/correction schedule locks still require CEO release. The original protected-field/append assertions were kept and pass in the frozen-source core run; the historical failing run remains recorded above.

Fresh participant creation omits blank fields instead of treating them as authored values. Profile updates retain explicit clears. An already prepared operation keeps its original ID and payload, including during recovery; the normalization change does not rewrite submitted intent or existing protection metadata.
